# Praktika's creator videos: catalogue and formats to recreate

Compiled 2026-10-10. Companion to [`praktika-teardown.md`](praktika-teardown.md).

## Read this before briefing anyone

- **None of these videos was watched.** TikTok, Instagram and YouTube were blocked from the research environment.
- **What we have:** links, verbatim captions, cached like and comment counts, and a newsletter's description of the breakthrough video.
- **What we don't have:** the beat-by-beat structure. It is *reconstructed*, not observed.
- **Before you brief creators, open the top 5 links below yourself.** Note length, the first 2 seconds, on-screen text and where the app appears.
- Dates are decoded from the video IDs.
- Like counts are cached snapshots and may be old.

## 2. The breakthrough video, as described in writing

Source: the Consumer Startups newsletter, quoted via search snippets.

- **The creator:** "Gustavo", a Brazilian English-teaching creator. He had **~10K followers but 2–3M views per video**.
- **The video:** "Gustavo talking to Praktika's AI avatar while **intentionally making ridiculous mistakes in English**."
- **The reach:** one video reportedly got **12M views on Instagram and 10M each on TikTok and YouTube**.
- **What Praktika did next:**
  - **Locked him in** with an exclusive content deal ("like an acquihire"). He wasn't monetising yet, so he accepted.
  - **Doubled down** on the format.
  - **Boosted the winners with paid ads.** The House of Marketers agency's account describes them as organic winners re-run as Spark Ads.
- **How the agency frames the format:** "a judgment-free space to be bad at a language."

**What isn't confirmed:**
- Which Gustavo video was the 12M one. The 634K-like TikTok (#2) is the best guess.
- That #2 actually uses the deliberate-mistake format.

**Contradiction:** CEO Adam Turaev's LinkedIn claims "half a billion organic views, zero paid distribution". That conflicts with the paid boosting above. Likely both are true at different times. Don't plan around zero paid spend being enough.

## 3. The six formats, ranked by evidence

| Rank | Format | Evidence | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Deliberate-mistake comedy with the AI.** The creator plays a bad learner on purpose; the AI reacts in character. | 12M+ views (reported), 635K likes | The viewer's fear of speaking badly becomes a joke. The patient AI *is* the value: practice with nobody judging. Demo and entertainment are the same thing. |
| 2 | **Famous teacher + "practise every day."** | 363K / 125K / 102K likes | Borrowed credibility and reach. The script barely matters. |
| 3 | **Comedy skit around the app.** "It's holding me captive", "it almost ruined my marriage". | 160–170K likes | Pure entertainment. The app is the plot device. |
| 4 | **Timed Q&A challenge.** "10 questions", "7 questions in 60 seconds". | 40–125K likes, repeated | Viewers play along and answer out loud. Very rewatchable, very duet-able. |
| 5 | **❌/✅ mistake typical of the viewer's nationality.** | 25–28K likes, plus a whole creator-run feed (@nesly.praktika) | Cheap and endlessly repeatable. Each one speaks to *one* nationality. |
| 6 | **Real-life scenario role-play.** Job interview, immigration officer. | 76K likes | "Rehearse before the real thing." |

### How the system around the videos was built

1. **Creators picked by views-to-followers ratio**, not follower count. Gustavo: 10K followers, millions of views.
2. **Exclusive deals with the creators who win.**
3. **Creator-run spin-off accounts.** For example @nesly.praktika (pinned clips at 2.6M and 5.3M), @praktika.jiyong, @amina.praktika. Local brand pages too: @praktikajp, @praktika_turkiye.
4. **A tracked link per creator**, so each creator's revenue is measured, not just their views.
5. **Paid boost only after a video wins organically.**
6. **Fans pulled into the product.** Jay Fujiwara's voice was put inside the app for a limited time ("#PraktikaJay").

## 4. Recreating them for Polish B1 (scripts for Russian-speaking creators)

### Ground rules: Praktika's mistakes, don't repeat them

- **Film only what the product really does.** Praktika's ads show avatars and live corrections the app doesn't have. That is a big part of why their Trustpilot rating is 3.6★.
  - Pre-launch, a working prototype is fine, even ChatGPT plus a good prompt. Faking a feature is not.
- **No NAWA or Państwowa Komisja logos.** Never say "official".
- **Never "you'll pass" or a guarantee.** Say "find out today which part you'd fail".
- **Every video ends in the same place:** the free 10-minute B1 readiness check, through the creator's own link or promo code.

### F1 — "Сдаю B1 с AI-экзаменатором… очень плохо" (the deliberate-mistake format)

*Recreates the reported Gustavo format.* The creator sits a mock B1 oral and plays the classic Russian-speaker mistakes on purpose. The AI examiner stays polite and in character. The punchline is the scorecard.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | On-screen text: «Решила сдать B1 по польскому с AI-экзаменатором. Ошибка.» Creator's face plus the screen. |
| 2–15 s | Examiner: «Proszę opowiedzieć o swoim mieszkaniu.» Creator: «Mam duży… dywan. Śpię na dywanie.» Pause; examiner, politely: «Na… dywanie?» (dywan = carpet, not a sofa.) |
| 15–25 s | Escalate with 1–2 more false friends («Pracuję w zawodzie» meaning "at the factory"; «Kupiłam to w sklepie… no, w grobie» as the punchline), or address the examiner with «ty» instead of «Pan/Pani». |
| 25–35 s | **Scorecard on screen**, broken down by section: *Mówienie 41/100 ❌ — не выполнено 2 из 3 пунктов задания.* Creator: «Ну хоть не за 730 злотых узнала.» |
| CTA | «Проверь, какую часть провалил бы ты — ссылка в профиле.» |

**Why it fits:** the shame of a 730 PLN failure gets defused by laughing at it. The scorecard is the real product working, because it catches the actual failure reasons examiners name.

### F2 — ❌/✅ «Ошибки русскоязычных на экзамене B1» (a series)

One mistake per video, 7–12 seconds, the same template every time. Good for a creator-run spin-off account in the @nesly.praktika style.
- ❌ «Mam dywan w salonie, siedzę na nim i oglądam TV» ✅ «…siedzę na kanapie» (dywan = carpet)
- ❌ «Idę do sklepu… to znaczy na cmentarz?» ✅ sklep = shop, a grave is *grób*
- ❌ «Ona ma urodę jak potwór» ✅ uroda = beauty, not "ugliness"
- ❌ «Ty możesz powtórzyć?» (to the examiner) ✅ «Czy może Pan/Pani powtórzyć?»
- ❌ «Kupiłam owoce: ziemniaki i marchewkę» ✅ those are *warzywa* (owoce = fruit)
- **End frame:** «Это из 1 000 проверенных ответов. Свою ошибку найдёшь тут ↓» Only use that line once the number is real.

**Fact-check every pair with a Polish teacher before filming.** One wrong correction in this audience costs more than ten good videos earn.

### F3 — «7 вопросов устного B1 за 60 секунд — отвечай вслух» (challenge)

- **On screen:** a timer and the AI examiner asking real B1-style oral questions, e.g. «Co robisz w weekend?», «Opisz swoje miasto», «Dlaczego uczysz się polskiego?».
- **The creator** answers some and leaves gaps for viewers to answer out loud.
- **End:** «Сколько ответил без паузы? Пиши в комменты.» Then the CTA.
- **Why it works:** it is made for duets and stitches, and comments fuel the algorithm.

### F4 — Скетч «AI-экзаменатор не отпускает меня» (comedy skit)

*Recreates Nesly's and Carolina's skits,* tied to a real failure reason: not covering every element of the oral task.
- Creator tries to end the session.
- Examiner: «Jeszcze punkt trzeci zadania.»
- Creator: «Я уже всё сказала!»
- Examiner: «Nie powiedziała Pani, dlaczego.» (You didn't say why.)
- Cut to 3 a.m., the creator still answering.
- **Caption:** «На настоящем экзамене за это снимают баллы. Тут хотя бы не отпускают 😅»

### F5 — «Через неделю собеседование на Карту Поляка» (scenario role-play)

*For Job B, pre-move people in Belarus.* The creator rehearses with the AI playing the consul: a short interview about roots, traditions and why they want the Karta.
- **Hook:** «Консул спросил меня про Wigilia, и я…»
- **CTA:** «Прорепетируй до настоящего собеседования.»
- **Only film this** if the product really supports a Karta Polaka mode. Otherwise drop it.

### F6 — «Мой польский после 2 лет в Варшаве» (self-deprecation)

*Recreates Praktika's 6.7M-view brand video.* Built to run on your own account, with no creator needed.
- **The setup:** a quick-cut montage of everyday fails: shop, ZUS office, pharmacy. Then «…а B1 нужен для ВНЖ через 3 месяца».
- **The pivot:** cut to the 10-minute readiness check and its result.

## 5. Finding and running the creators

1. **Shortlist 20 creators.** Russian- or Ukrainian-language accounts on Polish, Karta Polaka or moving to Poland, on TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Telegram. Record their **average views on the last 10 videos ÷ followers**, and rank by that.
2. **Test 3 creators × 1 format each** (F1 first, then F2), €50–150 per video. Give each a separate link or promo code.
3. **Measure, per creator:** views → readiness checks completed → paid. Views alone mean nothing.
4. **The winner gets** a series deal, ideally an exclusive one, plus an affiliate cut (Praktika pays 30%). Maybe also a spin-off account in the "nesly.praktika" style.
5. **Only then** put money behind the specific videos that already won organically, as Spark Ads or Instagram Partnership Ads.

**Precondition:** the 10-minute readiness check has to exist and work, even as a prototype, before the first video goes out. The video only sells it.
